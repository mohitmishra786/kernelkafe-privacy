# KernelKafe Publisher

**KernelKafe Publisher** is the private upload tool of the YouTube channel
**Kernel Kafe** ([youtube.com/@kernelkafe](https://www.youtube.com/@kernelkafe)), a channel of
educational explainers about systems programming.

It is a command-line program that only the channel owner runs, on their own computer, to
upload and schedule their own finished videos and Shorts on their own channel: title,
description, tags, thumbnail, caption file, playlist and publish time.

- **Who can use it:** only the owner of @kernelkafe. It has no other users and no sign-up.
- **What it accesses:** only the owner's own YouTube channel, after the owner signs in with
  Google (OAuth 2.0).
- **Source code:** [github.com/mohitmishra786/yt-setup](https://github.com/mohitmishra786/yt-setup)
  (MIT licence), see `cli.py upload` and `modules/publisher/`.
